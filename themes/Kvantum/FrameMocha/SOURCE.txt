FrameMocha - Kvantum theme for Qt 5 and Qt 6 apps (installed to ~/.config/Kvantum/FrameMocha)

Made from KvLibadwaitaDark, part of KvLibadwaita by GabePoel:
  https://github.com/GabePoel/KvLibadwaita (commit 1f4e0bec44b13dabfa1fe4047aa8eeaccf2f3557)
which is based on Colloid-kde by Vince Liuice (https://github.com/vinceliuice/Colloid-kde)
and KvAdapta by Tsu Jan. License: GPL-3.0, see LICENSE in this folder.

Changed by frameshell in October 2026 - the colours only, to Catppuccin Mocha
(catppuccin.com/palette; the shell's Mocha theme and its GTK theme Frame-Mocha): Mantle
windows and menus, Surface0 buttons and fields, Surface1 / Surface2 when hovered or
pressed, Mauve checkboxes, radio buttons, sliders and progress bars with Crust marks,
Text for text and arrows. The shapes are KvLibadwaita's. ../make-frame.py makes
FrameMocha.svg and FrameMocha.kvconfig from the original files.
