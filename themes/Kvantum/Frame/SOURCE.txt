Frame - Kvantum theme for Qt 5 and Qt 6 apps (installed to ~/.config/Kvantum/Frame)

Made from KvLibadwaitaDark, part of KvLibadwaita by GabePoel:
  https://github.com/GabePoel/KvLibadwaita (commit 1f4e0bec44b13dabfa1fe4047aa8eeaccf2f3557)
which is based on Colloid-kde by Vince Liuice (https://github.com/vinceliuice/Colloid-kde)
and KvAdapta by Tsu Jan. License: GPL-3.0, see LICENSE in this folder.

Changed by frameshell in October 2026 - the colours only, to the shell's and its GTK
theme's: #1a1a1a windows and menus, #313131 buttons and fields, grey selections, white
checkboxes, radio buttons, sliders and progress bars with dark marks. The shapes are
KvLibadwaita's. ../make-frame.py makes Frame.svg and Frame.kvconfig from the original files.
