FrameNoir - Kvantum theme for Qt 5 and Qt 6 apps (installed to ~/.config/Kvantum/FrameNoir)

Made from KvLibadwaitaDark, part of KvLibadwaita by GabePoel:
  https://github.com/GabePoel/KvLibadwaita (commit 1f4e0bec44b13dabfa1fe4047aa8eeaccf2f3557)
which is based on Colloid-kde by Vince Liuice (https://github.com/vinceliuice/Colloid-kde)
and KvAdapta by Tsu Jan. License: GPL-3.0, see LICENSE in this folder.

Changed by frameshell in October 2026 - the colours only, to the Noir colours (the
shell's Noir theme and its GTK theme Frame-Noir): black #000000 windows and menus,
#060606 buttons, fields and selections, a little lighter only when hovered or pressed,
white checkboxes, radio buttons, sliders and progress bars with black marks. The shapes
are KvLibadwaita's. ../make-frame.py makes FrameNoir.svg and FrameNoir.kvconfig from the
original files.
