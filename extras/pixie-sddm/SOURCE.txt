Changed by frameshell, to match the shell:
  theme.conf  the desktop wallpaper (assets/wallpaper.png, copied there by
              install.sh) and the shell's greys instead of Pixie's green
  Main.qml    four new theme.conf keys: accentTextColor, the colour of the
              arrow on the round button (Pixie: always white); two more,
              cornerColor (the date and the power icons and labels) and
              fieldColor (the password field's border and the round
              button), the accent when empty or with autoColor=true;
              errorColor, the card after a wrong password (Pixie:
              #442222); the date
              and the power icons sit on one line, each as far from the top
              as from its side of the screen (edgeGap, 38 px); the screen
              fades in from black and, after a successful login, back to
              black (the `curtain` rectangle); the card says "Caps Lock is
              on" (keyboard.capsLock) instead of "Num Lock is on", fading in
              and out
  components/PowerBar.qml  icons and labels centred by their ink, so
              they line up with the date
  components/Clock.qml     tabular digits (font feature "tnum"), so the
              hours stand exactly above the minutes; minutes in the
              hours' colour (white); theme.conf hourColor / minuteColor
              (empty or autoColor=true: the colour made from the accent)
Everything else is unchanged.
